Who I am

About

Foyez Ahmmed is a PhD candidate in biostatistics at the University of Kansas Medical Center, advised by Dr. Leonidas Bantis, defending Sep 23, 2026. His dissertation develops a generalized ROC framework for combining correlated biomarkers, and he will join the U.S. Food and Drug Administration as an ORISE Research Fellow in September 2026.

He is the author or co-author of 22 peer-reviewed journal articles spanning cancer biomarkers, maternal & child health, and mental health epidemiology, and a member of the American Statistical Association. Before his PhD, he was an Assistant Professor and Lecturer in Statistics at Comilla University, Bangladesh.
